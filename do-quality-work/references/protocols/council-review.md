# Protocol: Council Review

A council resolves consequential tradeoffs that focused evidence and normal review cannot settle. The [main skill](../../SKILL.md) selects the workflow. A request for a team or independent review alone is not a request for council ceremony.

## When useful

Use a small council when the user explicitly requests it, independent reviewers remain in substantive disagreement after evidence, or a costly-to-reverse cross-domain tradeoff cannot be resolved by the accountable owner. Important, public, or cross-platform work alone does not trigger it.

Choose only the two to five contributors whose expertise or decision rights can change the outcome. Partition lanes and disclose missing coverage. Multiple role lenses from one execution are one viewpoint, not independent votes.

## Review and resolution

Supply the exact artifact/revision, decision question, constraints, current evidence, prior focused findings, and remaining tradeoff. Each contributor states concrete risks, strongest credible alternative, evidence that would change their judgment, and recommended path. Include adversarial or failure analysis within these lanes only when relevant.

The accountable owner synthesizes evidence and tradeoffs, resolves supported blockers, records consequential dissent, and chooses within their authorization. Do not require a fixed agreement percentage. If the user explicitly asks for voting, report independently executed participants and apply the user's decision rule; absent a specified threshold, report votes as advice rather than inventing a gate. Votes never override a verified blocker or missing required evidence.

After two passes without new evidence, stop cycling. Preserve unresolved options and either perform an admissible non-production probe outside the blocked boundary or escalate the decision that exceeds the owner's authority. Do not present lack of consensus as proof that safe independent work must stop.

## Output

Keep a compact receipt: artifact/revision, decision, independent lanes, evidence, accepted rationale, material dissent, unresolved blockers, and next action. Record a durable decision only when it will guide future work. Do not produce a transcript or automatically launch red-team, steelman, or pre-mortem workflows from a council finding.
